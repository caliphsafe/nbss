02 / MARKETING BRIEF

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY

Current position

NBSS has an established local reputation and customer base. Its online presence is less developed than its in-store business.

Company history

Rasmus and Sarah Tonnessen were the original owners of New Bedford Ship Supply. Rasmus is associated with the development of New Bedford’s scallop fishery, and the company grew around the practical needs of fishing vessels and their crews.

The business later passed to their niece, Harriet Didriksen, who began working there at 13 and spent decades in the fishing industry. NBSS remains a family business; its official history describes three generations of ownership and staff members with more than 40 years of experience.

At 108 Front Street, near Pier 3, NBSS operates as a ship chandlery: a supplier of the gear, tools, provisions and everyday essentials needed aboard a vessel. The store serves commercial fishing crews and also welcomes other customers. Its apparel carries the identity of this working supply business.

Background: NBSS history · The chandlery · Family history · Local reporting

Apparel demand

The owner reports strong in-store clothing sales and ongoing demand from existing customers. The range includes branded hats, T-shirts and work layers.

Brand background

The company’s identity is tied to New Bedford, commercial fishing and generations of service to the waterfront. Its history, staff and customer relationships provide context for the apparel brand.

Online presence

An existing Shopify apparel store provides a starting point. The owner wants clothing and the brand to have greater prominence online, with consistent marketing to support the store.

Many current customers are fishermen who are less comfortable shopping online. Clear product information, straightforward ordering and accessible customer support are important considerations.

03 / MARKETING BRIEF

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY

Business goals

The project combines an improved online store with ongoing brand development and marketing.

Build recognition

Establish a consistent online presence that reflects NBSS’s history, products and connection to the fishing community.

Grow apparel sales

Make the clothing easier to discover and purchase online, serve existing customers and reach customers beyond the physical store.

Develop an ongoing business

Support repeat purchases, new apparel designs and a dependable customer experience through coordinated marketing, production and fulfillment.

04 / MARKETING BRIEF

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY

Project responsibilities

Abdou and Mamadou will manage the website, apparel development and store operations through MA2K.

Abdou · Website & creative

Design and launch the website; configure Shopify management; maintain the site and product catalog; develop the owner’s garment ideas; and provide design support for the brand.

Mamadou · Production & fulfillment

Produce garments through MA2K’s existing relationship with NBSS, including screen printing and embroidery. Manage quality checks, inventory coordination, packing, shipping and returns handling.

NBSS · Brand & approvals

Provide company and product information, access to the business and approval of brand direction, apparel designs and customer policies. The owner retains control of the brand and business.
