02 / MEET NBSS

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY · MA2K

A working supply house.
A brand people already wear.

New Bedford Ship Supply is a longstanding family business rooted in the fishing industry of New Bedford, Massachusetts. Its relationship with the waterfront comes from supplying the people who work there.

01

The business

Based on Front Street near the port, NBSS provides provisions, tools, equipment and apparel to the fishing community. Its history reaches back to 1939. The clothing comes from a real working business and an established local identity.

02

The apparel

The owner reports that NBSS clothing sells well in the physical store, with an ongoing customer base. Hats, tees and work layers already carry a name that customers recognize and choose to wear.

03

The online gap

NBSS has an existing Shopify apparel store, but the owner sees its online presence as underdeveloped. The strength of the in-store business has not yet been matched by a consistent online brand presence.

What we know today

The existing customer relationships and in-store apparel demand are the starting point. We have not established the size of the online opportunity or attributed results to any marketing program.

03 / WHY THE BRAND MATTERS

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY · MA2K

The work. The people.
The place.

NBSS has a story beyond its products: generations of service to a fishing community, a recognizable place in New Bedford and clothing that carries that connection.

01

An identity with roots

There is a real business, location and community behind the name. Its history and relationships give the brand something meaningful to draw from as it grows beyond the store.

02

A connection customers understand

For existing customers, NBSS can represent their work, family or hometown. That connection may also matter to people who no longer live locally or who discover the brand through others.

03

A story that is not fully reaching people

We see potential in bringing more of the company’s people, history and everyday life into view. The brand’s next phase presents an opportunity to translate that foundation into a distinctive online presence.

A reference for the ambition

Our work on Over The Rail is a reference point for the kind of apparel business the owner wants online. What stands out to us is the connection between the clothing and the people’s stories. NBSS has its own history and angle to develop.

04 / WHAT THE OWNER WANTS

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY · MA2K

Make apparel a bigger
part of the business.

The owner has asked Abdou and Mamadou to take responsibility for creating and operating the online apparel business—from the website and garment development through ongoing management and fulfillment.

01

An apparel-first website

Give clothing and the NBSS brand a more prominent online home, while preserving a clear connection to the established supply business. Make the store useful and approachable for its existing customers.

02

A dependable operation

Keep products, stock, customer questions and orders moving through one coordinated team. The owner wants the online store to function as an ongoing business, beyond the initial website launch.

03

A stronger presence

Help more people discover the brand, connect with it and choose its apparel. The goal is to develop the online side of a business that already has customer relationships in person.

A customer consideration

Many existing customers are fishermen who may be less comfortable shopping online. The experience needs to feel straightforward and familiar, with clear product information and access to real help.

05 / WHAT WE WILL BUILD

NEW BEDFORD SHIP SUPPLY · MA2K

The storefront.
The product. The operation.

We are bringing the digital and production work together through MA2K. Abdou leads the website and creative side; Mamadou leads garment production and fulfillment.

01

Website & Shopify · Abdou

Design and launch the apparel-led website, organize the catalog and product pages, and configure Shopify management. Build the brand and company pages, customer information and ordering experience needed to support the store.

02

Garment development · Abdou + Mamadou

Develop the owner’s ideas into artwork and production-ready designs. Mamadou handles screen printing, embroidery, sampling and production through the existing MA2K relationship with NBSS.

03

Order operation · Mamadou

Establish online inventory coordination, product checks, packing, shipping and returns handling. Connect production availability with what the store offers so the team can fulfill what customers order.

A foundation marketing can build on

The intention is to give the brand a functioning store, a source of new products and an operating team behind it. We want marketing to have a dependable business to bring customers into.
