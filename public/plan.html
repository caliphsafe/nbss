02 / THE OPPORTUNITY

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

An established name.
A stronger online future.

NBSS already has the history, recognizable apparel and an in-store customer base. We want a marketing partner who can help turn that foundation into consistent online discovery, purchases and repeat relationships.

01

Start with what is real

New Bedford Ship Supply has served the working waterfront for generations. The owner reports strong in-store apparel demand. Audit the existing Shopify store and channels before deciding what to keep, improve or replace.

02

Make apparel the lead

Build the online brand around clothing and the people who wear it. Preserve access to the commercial supply business while giving apparel a clearer identity and shopping journey.

03

Build a repeatable operation

Connect storytelling, product availability and fulfillment. The aim is a dependable online business supported by a content and campaign system the team can sustain.

Our starting evidence

Existing product imagery and company history establish the creative foundation. Online demand, channel performance and customer behavior still need to be validated. This plan does not assume a proven online audience.

03 / BRAND & AUDIENCE

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

People wear
a connection.

Position NBSS apparel as a connection to work, family and New Bedford. Let real customers and staff carry the story in their own words.

01

Working waterfront

Fishermen, crews and marine trades already understand the name. Show garments in authentic daily use; explain fit, care and practical details without making unsupported performance claims.

02

Families & gift buyers

Help relatives and friends choose confidently through sizing, gift suggestions, familiar products and clear ordering guidance. Make the relationship to the person wearing the gear visible.

03

New Bedford, near & far

Reach local supporters, former residents and people connected to the port. Test broader heritage-apparel interest gradually without diluting the company’s roots.

Voice and creative direction

Straightforward, human and specific. Use the counter, the waterfront, the garments and the people as the visual language. Avoid staged hardship, invented testimonials and generic lifestyle claims. Verify historical details with NBSS before publication.

04 / ONE OPERATING TEAM

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Clear ownership.
Shared execution.

MA2K brings creative development, digital operations and production together. The proposed marketing partner leads the marketing program in close coordination with Abdou and Mamadou.

01

Abdou · Creative & digital

Own website direction, Shopify setup, garment artwork and the connection between brand presentation and store operations. Supply creative assets, coordinate approvals and turn campaign needs into landing pages and product updates.

02

Mamadou · Production & fulfillment

Own garment production, printing and embroidery, quality checks, stock coordination, packing, dispatch and returns handling. Confirm product readiness and capacity before campaigns go live.

03

Marketing partner · Proposed lead

Develop channel strategy, editorial planning, content briefs, campaign execution, distribution and reporting. Recommend the production workflow and identify which tasks the partner delivers directly versus coordinates.

NBSS remains the decision maker

The owner approves brand direction, products, customer policies and public claims. NBSS retains its brand, store and channel accounts. Confirm the final responsibility matrix together before kickoff; the partner role described here is a proposed scope, not an existing commitment.

05 / WEBSITE & SHOPIFY

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Easy to discover.
Easy to order.

Relaunch an apparel-first mobile experience that feels as helpful as the store counter. Keep the ordering path straightforward for customers who are less comfortable shopping online.

01

Storefront & product pages

Build the home page, apparel collections, brand story, contact/help and policy pages. Begin with proven products and accurate variants, size guidance, care information, photographs and stock status. Keep marine-supply information easy to find.

02

Checkout & customer confidence

Use Shopify checkout, guest purchasing and clear delivery information. Show contact options and accessible labels. Offer pickup only after the store confirms a reliable handoff process. Test on small screens and common browsers.

03

Admin & measurement

Configure the existing Shopify admin for products, inventory, orders and fulfillment. Set staff permissions, analytics and consent-aware tracking. Check product metadata, search visibility, structured information and campaign landing pages.

Launch acceptance

Complete a test purchase, confirmation, dispatch notification, refund and stock adjustment. Check every live product variant, link and form. Document who responds to a customer question and how the team handles an unavailable item.

06 / APPAREL DEVELOPMENT

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Keep the favorites.
Develop what comes next.

Start with the garments customers already recognize. Use small, deliberate releases to learn what deserves to become a permanent part of the range.

01

Core assortment

Audit existing hats, tees and layers with NBSS. Choose the launch assortment from current demand, available stock and production readiness. Create a product sheet for each style with garment, artwork, variants, sizing and care details.

02

Owner ideas into artwork

Abdou translates the owner’s ideas into concepts and production-ready files. Present mockups, consolidate feedback, approve placement and colors, then confirm print or embroidery requirements with Mamadou.

03

Samples before campaigns

Check samples for fit, decoration placement, color consistency and photography readiness. Approve the final item before promising a launch date. Use customer feedback and repeat interest to guide future collections.

Creative capacity planning

Use three original garment concepts per month as an initial planning cadence, to be confirmed with the team. Keep garment artwork separate from social content deliverables. Maintain a shared queue showing brief, review, sample and release status.

07 / MARKETING STRATEGY

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Tell the story.
Make the next step clear.

Marketing should connect the people and place behind NBSS to products customers can actually buy. Every campaign needs a clear audience, a useful story and a ready destination.

01

Discover

Introduce the brand through customer portraits, waterfront stories, local collaborations and short videos. Lead with an authentic reason to care. Test which themes draw relevant attention rather than chasing broad reach alone.

02

Consider & purchase

Pair strong stories with product demonstrations, fit details, customer answers and a direct product or collection link. Ensure the advertised item is available and the destination matches the message.

03

Return & recommend

Build voluntary email relationships, share restocks and new chapters, request product feedback and feature customer stories with permission. Give existing buyers a reason to stay connected beyond the first order.

Bridge the counter and the website

Equip staff with a simple explanation of the online store, a readable web address and optional QR cards. Invite email signup without pressure. Track online orders from existing customers separately where possible so channel shifts are not mistaken for entirely new demand.

08 / CONTENT & PHOTOGRAPHY

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Real people.
A useful content library.

Create a coordinated photography and video program: one launch library, regular local capture and seasonal refreshes. Confirm the photographer’s deliverables and usage rights before production.

01

Launch photography

Create a shot list covering clean product fronts, backs and details; on-body fit; the store; the counter; staff portraits; and waterfront context. Identify every launch product and required crop. Agree final image counts, formats and delivery dates with the photographer.

02

Monthly capture

Plan a recurring local session around one person, one product and one timely story. Record short interviews, natural work details and product demonstrations. Capture vertical footage and stills that can support social, email and the website.

03

Editing & asset management

Organize originals and approved edits by subject, product and date. Deliver captioned videos, clean product images, accessible descriptions and approved copy. Maintain release permissions, credits, usage limits and a shared library.

Production workflow

Marketing partner writes the brief and shot list with Abdou; NBSS confirms access and participants; Mamadou prepares garments; the assigned creator captures; the editor delivers; Abdou and NBSS approve; the partner schedules. Agree who handles each production step before work begins.

Authenticity and access

Obtain permission from featured people and property or vessel owners. Follow site safety requirements and weather constraints. Never let a content session interrupt working crews or imply endorsements that have not been approved.

10 / CHANNELS & CAMPAIGNS

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Give every channel
a defined job.

Start with a focused channel mix and improve it through evidence. The marketing partner should recommend the final sequence after reviewing current accounts, assets and customer behavior.

01

Facebook & Instagram

Use organic posts, Reels and Stories to build familiarity and conversation. Respond to routine comments on business days; escalate order issues to the operations team and sensitive public questions to NBSS.

02

Advertising

Begin with a small number of clear audience and creative tests. Separate new-audience discovery from re-engagement when the audience is large enough. Confirm tracking, consent and landing-page readiness before activation; use tagged links and document each test.

03

Email & search

Set up voluntary signup, a welcome journey, relevant recovery messages and post-purchase follow-up within applicable permissions. Improve product titles, descriptions, FAQs and brand information so customers can find and understand the range.

Campaign launch checklist

Named audience and objective; approved creative and copy; correct product link; available sizes; tested mobile page; tracking checks; fulfillment readiness; owner approval. Agree any offer with NBSS first. Stop campaigns for unavailable products and review unclear or misleading messaging immediately.

Local relationships

Explore customer features, neighboring waterfront businesses and relevant community events. Agree permissions and deliverables before featuring collaborators. Avoid publishing a partnership or collaboration as confirmed until both parties approve it.

11 / STORE OPERATIONS

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

The promise continues
after checkout.

Marketing and fulfillment need one shared view of what is ready to sell, what is moving and what customers are asking.

01

Inventory & readiness

Keep a designated online stock record, reconcile it weekly and flag low quantities. Record incoming production, damaged units and campaign allocations. Mamadou confirms readiness before a product or restock announcement.

02

Orders & dispatch

Review cleared orders on business days, confirm stock, inspect and pack accurately, then provide tracking. Target dispatch within two business days for in-stock orders; confirm operating capacity and exceptions before launch.

03

Support & returns

Provide visible customer help and target an initial response within one business day. Document return eligibility, inspection, restocking and resolution in line with NBSS policies. Feed recurring sizing or product questions back into content and product pages.

Exception handling

When a stock discrepancy, delay or damaged item occurs, operations owns resolution and customer communication; marketing pauses affected messages. Production lead times are handled separately. Before major releases, confirm workload, receiving space and dispatch coverage.

12 / LAUNCH ROADMAP

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Prepare the foundation.
Then build momentum.

Target a four-to-six-week relaunch once access, product information, approvals and production availability are confirmed. Photography, inventory or approval delays may move the date.

01

Week 1 · Align & audit

Review Shopify, channel history, customer questions and existing assets. Agree audience priorities, roles, launch assortment, content pillars and approval workflow. Establish baseline measurements and prepare the shoot brief.

02

Weeks 2–3 · Build & capture

Develop the storefront and product catalog, capture launch photography, prepare initial content and configure customer journeys. Confirm tracking and create the first campaign destinations.

03

Weeks 4–6 · Test & relaunch

Run the checkout and operations checklist, complete approved content, confirm stock and train the team. Introduce the store to existing customers first, then activate the agreed discovery campaigns.

Launch gate

Do not announce a fixed public date until product availability, approved imagery, tested checkout, customer policies, account access and fulfillment coverage are all confirmed. The owner signs off on launch readiness.

13 / FIRST SIX MONTHS

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Learn together.
Improve deliberately.

The first phase establishes dependable execution and useful evidence. Review progress monthly and conduct a formal partnership evaluation at six months.

01

Days 1–30 · Establish the baseline

Publish consistently, confirm tracking accuracy and review early customer questions. Check orders and fulfillment daily on business days. Identify broken steps and improve them before expanding activity.

02

Days 31–90 · Refine the program

Compare story themes, formats and audience responses. Improve weak landing pages, develop helpful product content and identify repeat interest. Use one clear hypothesis per test and document what changed.

03

Months 4–6 · Strengthen what works

Build on proven themes, improve repeat-customer journeys and plan seasonal releases around capacity. Review the partner’s delivery, team coordination, channel performance and customer experience.

Six-month partnership evaluation

Agree what to continue, change or stop; reassess responsibilities, deliverables, production needs and operating capacity; then decide the next phase together. This is a planning milestone, not a promise of specific commercial results.

14 / REPORTING & DECISIONS

NBSS · MA2K · MARKETING PARTNER

Measure the work.
Learn from the response.

Use one shared reporting rhythm and clear metric definitions. Set targets after baseline data is reviewed; do not invent a success threshold before understanding the current store.

01

Audience & engagement

Track qualified website visits, reach, saves, meaningful comments, video completion, subscriber growth and email clicks. Distinguish genuine interest from broad exposure and vanity metrics.

02

Shopping & retention

Track product views, add-to-cart behavior, checkout completion, order counts, repeat purchase rate and product-level interest. Use consistent reporting windows and note stockouts that affect results.

03

Delivery & customer experience

Track planned versus published assets, approval turnaround, order dispatch time, support response time and return reasons. Review whether the operation is delivering what the marketing promises.

Weekly working check · Monthly owner review

The marketing partner maintains a concise dashboard and test log. Weekly: upcoming content, campaign observations, stock and blockers. Monthly: results, lessons, completed deliverables and next actions. Compare platform reporting with store data and tagged links; label attribution gaps instead of claiming every order came from advertising.
