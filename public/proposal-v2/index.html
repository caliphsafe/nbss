THE OPPORTUNITY

A real history.
An online next chapter.

Since 1939, New Bedford Ship Supply has served the fishing industry from the New Bedford waterfront. Its Front Street store connects provisions, tools and apparel with the crews and community who rely on them.

Strong in-store apparel demand and an established customer base provide the starting point. The online experience will bring that identity to existing customers, New England workwear buyers, fishing families and customers with a connection to the South Coast.

Apparel remains the primary focus, supported by a small, practical selection of supplies available to order through Shopify. Clear product information and straightforward purchasing will help customers who are less accustomed to shopping online.

MA2K IMPRESSION · $1,500 PER MONTH

Ongoing work.
Defined responsibilities.

MA2K owns the website, Shopify administration and garment design delivery. The monthly fee covers both execution and oversight of that work.

Website care

Check mobile and desktop navigation, product options, add-to-cart and the checkout handoff weekly. Address broken links, missing images and layout or integration issues within the existing website.

Shopify administration

Keep the online catalog organized and store settings aligned with approved operations. Coordinate availability updates using stock information supplied by NBSS; NBSS maintains physical inventory accuracy.

Product listings

Prepare descriptions, supplied images, pricing, options and collection placement for up to four apparel or supply listings. Verify the customer’s path from product selection to checkout.

Store merchandising

Refresh featured products and seasonal collections. Place approved campaign banners and update existing landing pages so advertising leads to relevant, accurate products.

Four garment designs

Develop NBSS’s ideas into four standard garment concepts each month, including routine color variations and two revision rounds each. Additional standard designs are $150 each, approved in advance; complex illustration is quoted separately.

Store performance

Review product-page engagement and purchasing friction. Make practical improvements within the existing site, such as clearer sizing, product information or navigation. Paid campaign optimization belongs to advertising management.

Technical support

Triage reported storefront problems and coordinate technical fixes. Routine delivery questions, returns and other order-related customer service remain with NBSS. This is not a 24-hour monitoring service.

Delivery oversight

Maintain a prioritized work list, coordinate approvals and report completed work and next priorities. Coordinate storefront changes needed for campaigns without duplicating paid ad management.

Weekly

Check the core shopping flow, review reported technical issues and confirm featured-product availability with NBSS.

As needed

Handle approved routine corrections and technical issues during business days. Prioritize work according to its effect on purchasing.

Monthly

Deliver the listing and design allowances, one batch of merchandising updates, a store-performance review and a prioritized round of improvements. Share a concise summary of completed work and next priorities.

Major redesigns, substantial new features and work beyond the agreed allowances require a separate approved quote.

Clear operational ownership

NBSS handles physical stock, picking, packing, dispatch, tracking updates, returns and order-related customer service. MA2K handles technical storefront issues and uses NBSS’s inventory information to support accurate online listings.
