Read the assumptions and exclusions +

What is included

Garment cost is the complement of the selected gross margin. Other allowances: payment fees 3.5%, net shipping subsidy 8%, packaging 2%, and unrecovered return / damage costs 3% of net sales. Fixed costs: $2,000 services + $300 content + $150 software + selected advertising. Fulfillment adds $4 for each order above 75 per month.

At the defaults, 38.5% of net sales remains before fixed costs and fulfillment overages. Break-even includes those overages and assumes the same order value and cost mix.

What it does not promise

Order volumes are illustrative inputs, not outputs caused by the ad budget. Changing advertising changes costs only. Growth depends on existing customers, organic reach, referrals, repeat buyers and effective paid acquisition.

Net sales exclude tax and customer shipping charges, and are after discounts and refunds. Shipping subsidy is carrier cost less customer shipping collected. The 3% allowance covers additional unrecovered costs, not refunds already deducted.

What needs validation

Assumes up to two items and one parcel per order. Extra designs, items, specialists, storage, receiving and support are excluded. Capacity above 200 orders/month must be reviewed and may add costs. Figures exclude income tax, owner compensation and existing NBSS overhead. Production purchases and cash timing may differ from garment costs recognized on sold units.

The traffic estimate uses an illustrative 2% store conversion rate; it is not a benchmark or established NBSS rate. Validate margins, customer demand, site traffic and acquisition costs before scaling.

Monthly services begin at relaunch and are paid in advance. Initial term: six months from relaunch at $2,000/month ($12,000 in management fees). Meet before the term ends to reevaluate results, workload, marketing budget, scope and pricing. Renewal or revised terms require mutual written agreement; there is no automatic fee increase or renewal. Variable charges are itemized. A signed agreement governs delivery and early termination.
