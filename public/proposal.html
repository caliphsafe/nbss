Scope, working terms and source notes +

Working terms

Monthly services begin at relaunch and are paid in advance. Initial term: six months from relaunch at $2,000/month ($12,000 in management fees). Meet before the term ends to reevaluate results, workload, marketing budget, scope and pricing. Renewal or revised terms require mutual written agreement; there is no automatic fee increase or renewal. Variable charges are itemized. A signed agreement governs delivery and early termination.

NBSS funds postage and third-party spending. The unspent content reserve remains NBSS money and carries forward. No vendor markup is assumed. MA2K corrects its confirmed packing or dispatch errors at its expense.

Scope boundaries

Monthly website care includes up to 4 hours and up to 4 new or revised listings within those hours. New features, custom admin software, complex artwork and third-party inventory integrations are separately scoped. The build configures Shopify’s existing admin.

NBSS supplies accurate product/history information, approvals, staff access and permissions for photography. Target approvals within three business days. On exit, hand over access, operating records, outstanding orders and stock counts.
