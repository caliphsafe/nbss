01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The store already sells. Online is the gap.

NBSS has the three things most apparel brands spend years trying to build: a real place, a real customer, and product you cannot buy anywhere else. Its online presence is simply less developed than the business behind it. An existing Shopify store gives us the starting point. Consistent marketing is what turns it into a second storefront.

What we are selling, and why it travels

The range already mixes identity and utility: shackle and heritage ship logo trucker hats, the classic beanie, crewneck tees, joggers, and heavy work layers including NBSS x Carhartt hoodies and zip styles. Hats at $34 and a beanie at $25 are easy first purchases and gifts. Layers and Carhartt pieces carry the higher basket. Every item says where it came from, which is exactly what makes it work beyond Front Street.

Core: the fleet and the waterfront

Commercial fishermen, crews, boat owners, and dock and marine trades in New Bedford and across New England. They already trust the product because they use it. Ads here drive repeat purchase, crew and boat orders, and easy reordering without a trip to the store.

Workwear buyers nearby

Trades, outdoor workers, and cold-weather buyers across Massachusetts and New England who buy Carhartt-grade layers for work, not fashion. Lead with warmth, durability, and fit.

Heritage and hometown buyers, nationally

People from New Bedford and the South Coast now living elsewhere, fishing families, and buyers who collect authentic regional workwear. This is the growth audience: the hat becomes a hometown signal.

Gift, visitor, and discovery buyers

Waterfront visitors, maritime and fishing enthusiasts, and gift buyers in peak seasons. Lower intent at first, strong for hats, tees, and limited drops in peak seasons.

One caution built into the plan

Many core customers are less comfortable shopping online. So ads cannot do the job alone. Product pages need clear sizing, materials, and shipping information, plain ordering, and visible customer support. We will flag any page that is losing paid traffic before we scale spend behind it.

3. Convert and recover

Channels and tactics

Retargeting on Meta, TikTok, and Google for product viewers, cart abandoners, and past buyers. Clear offer, shipping, and support messaging.

What it does for NBSS

Turns interest into orders and brings in-store customers back online for reorders and gifts.

Starting budget shape

Until test results say otherwise, start near 55% discovery video, 25% search and shopping, 20% retargeting. We rebalance weekly toward whatever is returning, and we pause what is not. These shares are a starting point, not a promise.

Hero products first

Launch with a tight set, not the whole catalog: the shackle trucker hat, the classic snapback, the beanie for cold weather, a core tee, and one NBSS x Carhartt layer as the premium anchor. Five products make creative testing readable. Winners earn more products behind them.

04 / CREATIVE AND CONTENT

Real people, real work, real product. That is the creative.

NBSS should never look like a generic apparel ad. The creative advantage is that the product is actually worn at work, in weather, on boats. We shoot and cut for that, then test it honestly against real work and real weather.

Core ad creative (first shoot)

  • On the waterfront: crew wearing hats and layers at the store, on the pier, and on deck
  • Product proof: warmth, rain, fit, and wear over time, shown, not claimed
  • Heritage in 15 seconds: since 1939, three generations, one street in New Bedford
  • Simple product spots: one hat, one layer, price, and where to get it

Ad creative testing

  • Three angles per hero product, so we learn fast what buyers actually respond to
  • The waterfront story and the product proof in the first three seconds of every video
  • Winners earn more spend. Ads that do not return get paused, not defended
  • New versions cut from the same shoot, so testing does not need a new production every time

Campaign requirements

  • Product priorities, margins, stock levels, and any items that should not be promoted
  • Access to the Shopify catalog and product feed, Meta, TikTok, and Google ad accounts (owned by NBSS), and pixel and conversion tracking
  • Existing photography and video, plus one half-day shoot at the store and waterfront
  • Brand approvals, shipping and returns policy, and a named contact for fast creative sign-off
